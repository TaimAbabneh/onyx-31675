FTC EXPLAINED

How a match works.

Students design, build and program a robot for a new game each season. In BIOBUZZ, control changes hands during the match.

0:30

Autonomous

The robot follows its program. A team can use sensors to find pollen, move into position and score before the drivers take over.

2:00

Driver-controlled

Drivers take the controls and coordinate with their alliance partner to tip the hive and compete for flowers.
