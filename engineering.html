Pollen intake

How do you pick up a ball from the floor and keep it from rolling back out? One of our students worked on that question in Onshape.

How it works

The proposed intake fits inside an 18-inch cube. A ball enters at floor level, travels up a ramp and drops into a bin with room for more than one ball.

Ramp
The slope guides the ball under the wheels. The gap above the ramp determines where the wheels first touch it.
Flex wheels
Two soft wheels bend slightly around the ball as they turn, gripping it and pulling it uphill.
Gate and bin
The ball passes over a small lip and drops into storage. The lip keeps it from rolling back onto the ramp.

What to test next

Start with one ball to see whether the wheels catch it. Then feed a few in a row and watch the entrance to the bin. Does the first ball stay put when the next one comes through?
