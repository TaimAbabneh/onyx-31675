Pollen intake

A student’s Onshape design for BIOBUZZ. The intake fits inside an 18-inch cube.

How it works

Ramp
Guides the ball from the floor to the wheels.
Flex wheels
Two soft wheels grip the ball and pull it up the ramp.
Gate and bin
The ball drops over a lip into the bin. The lip keeps it from rolling back out.
